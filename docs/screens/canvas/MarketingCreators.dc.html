Creator FAQ

Before you start

Do I need to show my face?−

No. Your ID check is private and only used to confirm you are an adult. What you post is up to you.

Can I post adult content?+

Yes, if you are verified. Adult posts are shown only to fans who have also confirmed they are 18+.

What if someone else appears in my post?+

Tag them. They verify their ID and approve that post before it can go live, and they can be paid a share.

Can I change my prices later?+

Yes. Change your subscription price, bundles and post prices from your studio at any time.
